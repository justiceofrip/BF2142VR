BATTLEFIELD 2142 VR - DEFAULT CONTROLS

Names A/B/X/Y below correspond to primary/secondary buttons on Touch-style
controllers. VR CONTROLS > NEXT offers gun/vehicle options and two rebinding
pages. Inputs available for binding depend on your OpenXR runtime.

Right trigger: fire; click a menu with the laser.
Right grip while holding equipment: click to holster it.
Right grip while empty: squeeze the hand; it does not recall the last gun.
Left grip near rifle foregrip: support with both hands.
Keep squeezing to retain support when bringing the gun into line. To restore
distance release, choose it in AIM + VEHICLE COMFORT and adjust its radius.
Left grip while cupping your right hand: stabilize a pistol/revolver.
Bring a supported sight to either eye: automatic ADS, no button required.
Knife: draw from the chest, let the grip settle, then swing/stab or press right
trigger for a strike. Release before the next trigger strike. Native reach,
cooldown and hit rules still apply. Trigger strikes can be disabled.
Grenade: right trigger throws. The trajectory guide currently has known issues.
Thumb/index touch, grip and trigger animate fingers; no bare-hand tracking.

Left stick: move. Click: sprint.
Right stick: turn. Click: recenter. Keyboard Home also recenters.
A: jump / native parachute action. B: menu/back.
X: reload. Y: interact / enter / exit a vehicle.
Left trigger + X/Y while on foot: equipment cycling.
Left trigger + A while on foot: pick up a fallen kit (stock G action).
Stand close to the dropped kit; hold the combination briefly for native input.
Left trigger + right grip: open the native deployment/loadout screen.
After death: release right grip, then squeeze it to open deployment.

AIM CALIBRATION AND REBINDING
VR CONTROLS > NEXT > GUN PITCH: negative values lower the muzzle. Two-hand aim
still follows the positions of your hands. RESET GUN ANGLE restores the original.
On CONTROLLER BINDINGS, select an action, release all buttons, then press its
new button. For a chord, hold left trigger and press the second button. An
identical existing binding is cleared. RESTORE DEFAULT BINDINGS resets all ten.
Bindings cover jump, reload, use, kit pickup, deploy, sprint, recenter, menu and
equipment cycling. Menu laser/back, physical grips and fire retain their normal
controls. Extra hardware buttons must be exposed by the runtime first.

BODY SLOTS
Main weapon: behind right shoulder. Pistol: right hip. Knife: chest.
Utility slots: left belt, rear left and rear right. Grenade: front belt.
Move your right hand to the slot and press grip to draw.

MOVEMENT
Physically lower your head about 30 cm to crouch or 88 cm to prone.
Stand normally and choose CALIBRATE STANDING HEIGHT in VR CONTROLS if needed.
Physical stance can be disabled for seated play. Head-relative and left-
controller-relative locomotion are selectable. Missing controller direction
falls back to head direction. Snap/smooth turning and snap angle are adjustable.
Automatic parachute deployment uses the native game's fall/deployment rules.
On a ladder, hold either grip and pull down to ascend, up to descend; release
stops. Left-stick up/down remains available. Ladder hands are still a work in
progress rather than individual physical rung grips.

VEHICLES AND PODS
Hold left trigger + tap X: driver/F1.
Hold left trigger + tap Y: cycle seats 2-8; tap again for occupied/absent seats.
Y alone still enters/exits. Right trigger fires; right grip is alternate fire.
APC passenger/pod seat: right grip launches its pod.
Titan: Y enters the pod launcher; right trigger launches (right grip also works).
Movable stock guns follow head aim within native traverse limits. Aircraft
pilot guns stay fixed to aircraft direction; looking does not steer flight.
Nekomata main gun follows head pitch; steer its hull for horizontal aim.
The vehicle HUD now follows vehicle/gun aim. AIM + VEHICLE COMFORT can switch
it back to head-follow. VEHICLE: COCKPIT FOLLOW retains chassis pitch/roll;
LEVEL HORIZON remains the default. Head movement stays independent in both.

MENUS
Look directly at the left wrist for 0.4 seconds to reveal the smaller DEPLOY
button; point the right-hand laser at it and
pull the trigger. The native spawn/loadout screen opens on the larger panel.
Release trigger before selecting a spawn point. This button does not open VR
settings. Commander-specific shortcuts and right-click orders are future work.
Point your right controller at the menu and pull right trigger to select.
B opens/backtracks the game menu. VR CONTROLS opens the mod's settings panel.
Keyboard Insert can open/close VR CONTROLS if needed.

OPTIONAL DESKTOP SIMULATOR
F1 hold, F2 sight, F3 back slot, F4 pistol, F5 knife, F6 left belt,
F7 rear left, F8 grenade, keypad 0 rear right.
Right Ctrl grip, Right Shift support, F9 trigger, F10 menu, F11 tracking loss.
Mouse aims the menu laser; F9 clicks. Brackets turn head; PageUp/Down tilts it.
Keypad 7/9 lowers/raises head; End resets head pose; Home recenters.
Keypad 4/6, +/- and 8/2 move right hand; Decimal speeds movement.
Keypad 1/3 simulates right index/thumb touch; left Ctrl/Alt simulates left touch.
Left Shift is left trigger; Left Shift + X/Y selects vehicle seats.
The simulator must have window focus. Use Play VR.cmd for actual VR.

BATTLEFIELD 2142 VR 0.2.0-beta.1

Multiplayer beta: tracked head/arms/fingers/items, coherent observer smoothing,
body follow, cosmetic self-collision and fist-bump haptic implementation.
VR includes the same remote-player receiver shipped in the separate flat addon.

Latest fixes: stronger equipment pulses and left-hand crate cues, observer-only
native arm-recoil isolation and improved arm-segment clearance. Automated tests
pass; those changes have not had another visual/haptic session. Existing torso
calibration remains; some body/shoulder jank is still present.

Includes community controller aiming, MP ADS/movement/snap fixes, left-hand
crate throws, Titan/silo marker suppression, signed downloads and encrypted
pose/audio transport. Proximity voice worked with the second vrtester client;
separate-PC microphone routing/quality still needs broader testing.

Preserves stereo/6DoF, tracked hands/weapons, physical ADS/stance, two-hand grips,
body holsters, laser UI, floating HUD, walker lobby and vehicle/traversal work.
No manual reloads, physical steering wheels, knife hand-to-hand transfer or new
cockpits in this main beta. Separate SP experiments remain parked.
Stock v1.51 required; Remaster and full collision-aware roomscale unverified.

Both native builds/71 CTests and 279 community checks pass. See release CHECKS.

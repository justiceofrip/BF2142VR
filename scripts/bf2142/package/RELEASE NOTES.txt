BATTLEFIELD 2142 VR 0.1.0-alpha.1 - v30 PLAYTEST PACKAGE

An early PC VR alpha candidate. Multiplayer support and remote IK are pending.

Includes the accepted v29 loading-memory fix and v30 vehicle/parachute work:
head aiming for movable vehicle guns, controller seat selection and tracked
hands while gliding. Stock turret/aircraft limits remain; see CONTROLS.txt.
Preserves tracked weapons/hands, two-hand support, physical ADS, body holsters,
empty hands, controller-touch finger poses, physical stance, snap/smooth turning,
head/controller locomotion, laser menus, floating HUD and the walker hangar.

Setup generates the repaired weapons, body equipment and lobby locally. Game
files are not included. It installs the VR runtime under your game/BF2142VR,
backs up the original weapon archive and enables large-address support only if
needed, with a recorded backup for uninstall. The Hub/OpenSpy patch is separate.

This package's installer/load checks are recorded in PACKAGE CHECKS.txt.
Compilation and automated checks do not establish compatibility with every
headset, GPU or game modification. New interactions remain open for feedback.

No manual reloads, physical steering wheels, hand-to-hand knife transfer,
networked waving or new cockpit geometry are included yet.

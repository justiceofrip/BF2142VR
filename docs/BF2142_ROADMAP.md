# BF2142 VR roadmap

Current release: **0.2.0-beta.1**. See [changes since alpha](BF2_POST_ALPHA_UPDATES.md) and [known limits](BF2142_BETA_READINESS.md).

## Near term

- Broaden proximity voice device/routing tests on separate PCs; verify native squad/commander radio configuration. Two-client proximity speech has been confirmed.
- Confirm bilateral fist feedback and latest equipment cues, observer recoil isolation and arm clearance.
- Polish torso/shoulder/finger appearance across classes, weapons and stances.
- Integrate optional flat-addon download/updates into Reclamation Hub. Standalone signed join helper is ready; stock join alone cannot install native extensions.
- Expand internet crossplay, map coverage and dedicated-server performance tests. Flat addon is needed for tracked-player visuals and custom proximity chat.

## Experiments and longer term

- Review/merge the parked native-blue-sight and manual-reload experiments. Keep manual reloading optional where gun animation and ammo accounting support it.
- Physical vehicle controls including buggy steering wheels; cockpit rebuilding where practical.
- Hand-to-hand knife transfer, magazine grip polish and better native hand feel.
- Roomscale movement/collision testing: no-stick walking, walls/leaning, recenter, stance and observer appearance. Deferred at owner's request; physical stance alone is not full roomscale collision.
- Remaster and additional headset/runtime compatibility.

Generate weapon/model assets from each player's owned game. Do not redistribute them in mod downloads or BF2 source transfers.

**Battlefield 2142 VR - first public alpha in preparation**

I've been bringing Battlefield 2142 into VR, and the current development build
is now playable with motion controllers. Here's some footage and where the
project stands.

**Currently in the development build**

- Stereoscopic VR with 6DoF head tracking: look around and lean naturally.
- Tracked controller aiming, visible hands and local arm IK.
- Two-handed rifle support and pistol/revolver stabilization by cupping the
  firing hand with your off-hand.
- Physical ADS on supported weapons: bring the sight to your eye to activate
  the scope or reflex sight.
- Body equipment slots for your main weapon, sidearm, knife, grenades and
  utilities. Holster your weapon and keep both hands empty.
- Swing/stab knife attacks and straightforward trigger-operated grenade throws.
- Physical crouching and prone based on calibrated headset height.
- Smooth or snap turning, head-relative or left-controller-relative movement,
  recentering, and an in-VR controls menu.
- Floating HUD, optional crosshair, and laser-operated menus and spawn selection.
- Repaired first-person weapon surfaces for viewing guns from VR angles.
- A stereo hangar lobby featuring the EU and PAC walkers.
- Early controller-touch finger poses, grip-and-pull ladder climbing, and
  automatic parachute deployment during sustained falls. Ladder/parachute
  camera stabilization is implemented; these newer interactions need broader
  player feedback.

**Planned next**

- Verify multiplayer controller aiming and server-side hit registration.
- Explore a community Titan server and scheduled VR matches.
- Networked head/hand poses and third-person IK so players can see each other
  aim, gesture and wave.
- Grab and turn vehicle steering wheels with your hands, starting with buggies;
  investigate physical controls for other vehicles after that.
- Optional manual reloading, if it can work reliably with the native weapons.
- Further hand, finger, weapon-grip, optic and grenade-guide polish.
- Check Project Remaster/HD-mod compatibility and more headset/runtime setups.

**Longer-term experiments**

Hand-to-hand knife transfers, rung-specific climbing hands, and improved VR
cockpit models. These are ideas to explore, with no
promised release dates.

**Current limits and availability**

Singleplayer/bots is the current play-tested focus. Online VR gameplay is not
verified yet, and other players cannot see your tracked hands or waving in
this build. The tested setup is Quest 3 through Steam Link and SteamVR on
Windows; other setups still need testing. This is a PC VR mod requiring your
own working Battlefield 2142 v1.51 installation.

The first public download is being prepared as **0.1.0-alpha.1**. It is not
available yet; this post shows the development build. Expect an early alpha
with bugs and rough edges.

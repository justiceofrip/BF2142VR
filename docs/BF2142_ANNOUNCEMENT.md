**Battlefield 2142 VR - early PCVR alpha**

6DoF VR, motion-controlled weapons, physical ADS, body holsters, tracked hands and snap turning. Tested in singleplayer with Quest 3 + SteamVR.

**Download:** https://github.com/justiceofrip/BF2142VR/releases/tag/v0.1.0-alpha.1

**Quick install**
1. Get your own **BF2142 v1.51** working; use [Reclamation Hub](https://battlefield2142.co/) for the login fix.
2. Extract the **VR player ZIP**.
3. Run **Setup.cmd** and select **BF2142.exe**.
4. Connect your headset to SteamVR, launch **Play VR.cmd**, and load a bot match.

[Source + BF2 porting guide](https://github.com/justiceofrip/BF2142VR)

Planned: multiplayer testing, networked IK, physical steering and optional manual reloads. Multiplayer and final headset acceptance of this exact package remain unverified.
